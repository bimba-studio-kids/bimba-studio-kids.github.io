Privacy policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026.

Who we are

Bimba Studio publishes the YouTube channels Bimba Sing-Along (@bimbasingalong) and Bimba Comptines (@bimbacomptines). Contact: bimbastudio.kids@gmail.com.

Our publishing application

We use an internal application, “Bimba publication”, that connects to the YouTube Data API to upload videos to our own two channels and to update their description and banner. It is used only by our team, on our own Google account. It does not offer any service to other people and no outside user signs in to it.

Data we access

With our own authorization, the application accesses our own YouTube channels only: it uploads our videos, sets their title, description, tags, thumbnail and settings, and updates our channel branding. Access tokens are stored only on our team's computers and are never shared or sold.

Children and viewers

Our videos are made for children and declared as “made for kids” on YouTube. We do not collect, store or process any personal information about viewers, children or anyone else. Viewing our videos on YouTube is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

Google API Services

Our use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Our application uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service. You can revoke the application's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Changes

We will update this page if our practices change.
